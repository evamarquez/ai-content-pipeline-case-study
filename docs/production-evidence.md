# Production evidence

## Measured results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Visible pipeline stages | 12 |
| Articles produced | 549 |
| Jobs processed | 882 |
| Median keyword-to-document time | 4.7 minutes |
| Measured failure rate | Reduced from approximately 25-40% to 4-8% |
| Human control | Editorial review remained in the loop |

## What the evidence supports

The evidence supports a conclusion about workflow throughput, traceability, and measured reliability after asynchronous processing, queueing, retries, and stage-specific fixes.

## What it does not support

These figures do not prove traffic, search ranking, backlinks, citations, or revenue. Those require separate instrumentation and evidence.

## Audit discipline

When an evaluation score looked reassuring but did not measure the intended quality, the evaluator was corrected and representative scores became less flattering. The measurement became more useful because the claim and the test were aligned.
