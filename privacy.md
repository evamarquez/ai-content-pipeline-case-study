# Privacy boundary

This case study describes contracted internal work at an aggregate level. It is intentionally not a reconstruction of the private repository.

## Published

- The general problem and workflow shape.
- Aggregate throughput and reliability metrics.
- High-level architecture and design decisions.
- Operational lessons about retries, quality gates, and measurement.
- Security practices described without exposing implementation details.

## Not published

- Client names, domains, products, markets, or brand profiles.
- Article text, prompts, research briefs, source lists, or editorial feedback.
- Source code, database schema, server-function names, internal URLs, or deployment configuration.
- Credentials, tokens, service-account details, record IDs, screenshots, and private documents.
- Any claim about client SEO performance or revenue. The work is described through time saved, reliability, and operational throughput.

## Why this boundary exists

The system was built for an employer and its clients. The portfolio can demonstrate systems thinking and delivery judgment without publishing the implementation or making the work copiable.
